About GreenLoop

We make it simple for neighbours to organise, find, and join community clean-ups.

The problem

Many neighbourhoods have litter hotspots but no easy way to coordinate action. People want to help, but they don't know when events happen, where to go, or who to contact. Local councils and community groups lack a simple, shared place to advertise clean-ups.

Our solution

GreenLoop is a lightweight, accessible hub that lists upcoming clean-ups, explains what volunteers need to bring, and provides a fast registration form. It works on any phone, requires no login, and gives coordinators a clear way to collect sign-ups.

Who it's for

  • Residents who want to improve their area
  • Community groups and schools
  • Local councils coordinating volunteers
  • Families looking for safe, supervised activities

Our values

  • Accessibility first
  • No accounts, no data collection
  • Real, local impact

What we provide

Event listings

Filterable upcoming clean-ups with dates, locations and difficulty.

Simple registration

A validated form with instant confirmation — no password required.

Impact tracking

Live counters show how many bags and volunteers the community has logged.
